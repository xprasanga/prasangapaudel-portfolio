A brief introduction

A quantitative researcher and a learner.

I am a PhD student in Agriculture and Applied Economics at Texas Tech University. My academic work has given me a foundation in economic theory, econometrics, statistics, survey design, and applied research, while my projects have made me increasingly interested in the tools used to turn research questions into useful evidence.

R and Python are central to my current analytical workflow. I am also beginning a broader programming path through C++ and Java. This portfolio is designed to grow alongside that transition: it will contain research, programming exercises, visualizations, machine-learning work, and ongoing projects rather than only finished academic work.
