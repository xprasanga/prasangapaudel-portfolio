About the portfolio

A quantitative researcher learning to build with data.

I am a PhD student in Agriculture and Applied Economics at Texas Tech University. My academic work has given me a foundation in economic theory, econometrics, statistics, survey design, and applied research, while my projects have made me increasingly interested in the tools used to turn research questions into useful evidence.

R and Quarto have been central to my existing analysis and reproducible research workflow. I am also developing my Python skills and beginning a broader programming path through C++ and Java. This website is designed to grow alongside that transition: it will contain research, programming exercises, visualizations, and projects rather than only finished academic work.
